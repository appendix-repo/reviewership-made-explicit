**bs:E1. Main contribution.** In your reading, what does the manuscript claim as its main contribution? The abstract offers several candidates. Please rank them, merge them or replace them:
- `bs:C1` **Theory.** Interoperability standards make IT functions modular, "lowering the effective asset specificity of the hospital–vendor relationship" (`inv:abs.s3`). The model predicts a positive effect that is stronger where uncertainty is high (`inv:abs.s4`).
- `bs:C2` **Empirical regularity.** Modularity "strongly predicts the share of its IT spending that is outsourced" in a panel of 412 hospitals, 2009–2019 (`inv:abs.s5`).
- `bs:C3` **Adjudication between rivals.** Once modularity is in the model, peer adoption "no longer predicts outsourcing" (`inv:abs.s6`). So the results "rule out mimetic isomorphism as a driver of IT outsourcing in this setting" (`inv:abs.s7`).
- `bs:C4` **Reinterpretation.** A practice "often read as institutional fashion is, on closer inspection, economizing" (`inv:abs.s7`).
- `bs:C5` **Priority.** The paper claims to be "the first study to adjudicate directly between transaction-cost and institutional explanations of IT outsourcing" (`inv:abs.s8`).

Why I ask: each candidate is judged by different standards. C1 is judged by standards for theory and derivation. C2 and C3 are judged by standards for causal identification and evidence. C5 is judged by checking it against the literature. Your ranking decides which dimension the reviewer and I take first.

If other reviewers cover some of these areas, for example the institutional side, please say which. That tells me where this reviewer is expected to carry the weight.
